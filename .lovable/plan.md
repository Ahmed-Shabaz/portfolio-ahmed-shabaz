## Experience
- Create a dark technical visual direction with crisp typography, high-contrast accents, subtle grid details, and restrained motion.
- Lead with Shabaz Ahmed’s name, Automation Engineer role, experience summary, and clear links for email, LinkedIn, and CV download.
